VS Code video walkthroughs ​

Watch a short passage beside the written steps. Each recording below is a contiguous passage from the full tutorial with the same English narration, music and captions. The portrait crop focuses the fields used at that step. Use the player controls to seek, enable English captions or open full screen.

The videos record real native VS Code interfaces and Julia workers in isolated qualification workspaces. Their recorded-build captions identify the exact VSIX and Core source used. These recordings illustrate individual exercised actions; consult the qualification notes for the release and platform scope.

The 1.0.1 integration is being qualified. The public 1.0.0 extension and the candidate Pluto workflow have separate installation prerequisites. The full tutorial and twenty short passages are available below. Their footage and exports have passed review; the recorded-build captions retain each passage's provenance.

Full tutorial ​

The 29-minute walkthrough follows environment selection, measurements, plots, Pluto notebooks and a reviewed implementation. English captions are available in the player. Use the chapter timings to find a step. The tutorial combines recorded candidates; it does not establish that every feature passed on every platform. Individual captions and the qualification notes identify that scope.
